Period-Overlap Note

This source has been copied from United States 1946-89 into the United States 1990–present wiki because Chapters 1–4 and 47–62 cover the 1999 Sun Valley tech-bubble confrontation, the Salomon Brothers crisis and Buffett’s reformer turn, 1990s global deal/philanthropy arcs, and Susie’s death / claim-check giving through 2008—core post–Cold War finance and celebrity-capital material. Overview

The Snowball is Alice Schroeder’s authorized life of Warren Buffett. For this United States 1990–present wiki the usable core is the late frame and aftermath of empire: bubble skepticism at Sun Valley, Salomon’s near-death and congressional testimony, lottery-scale fame, the Genie fable taught to students, and the Gates-era philanthropy turn after Susie’s death. Period-gate hard on partnership-founding chapters except as continuity.

Book structure: Parts One–Six / Chs. 1–62; Afterword; Notes; Acknowledgments.
