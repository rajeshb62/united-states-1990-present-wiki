Overview

Breaking Through is Karikó’s first-person life-in-science: postwar Kisújszállás; University of Szeged and Biological Research Center work on RNA/interferon; immigration to Temple and then Uniformed Services University; decades at the University of Pennsylvania as a soft-money outsider synthesizing mRNA; partnership with Drew Weissman on nucleoside-modified, non-immunogenic mRNA; daughter Susan Francia’s Olympic rowing career as parallel spine; departure to BioNTech; and the 2020–21 transformation when Pfizer/BioNTech (and Moderna) mRNA COVID vaccines made the obscure platform world-historical.

PDF structure (from Contents): Preface; Prologue; six Parts (A Butcher’s Daughter; An Extremely Brief Interlude on Science; A Sense of Purpose; An Outsider Inside the System; Susan’s Mom; A Changed World); Epilogue; Photo Insert; Acknowledgments; About the Author. Note: pdftotext preserves Preface/Prologue/Epilogue headers but does not emit the six Part title pages in the body, so Part line ranges below are inferred from narrative transitions and should be treated as approximate mining guides.

Narrative Source Flow

Preface

Lines 48–84. Thank-you frame: daughter Susan’s second-grade teacher note prompts Karikó’s belated thanks to teachers and mentors from Hungary through cardiology/neurosurgery/immunology collaborators; seed metaphor for educators.

Prologue

Lines 85–169. Lab-bench tableau: forty years of pipettes and centrifuges vs. inner detective work; cell-as-city metaphor (factories, codes, surveillance); sets the memoir’s claim that “boring” bench science is where breakthroughs begin.

Part One — A Butcher’s Daughter (approx. L170–1749)

Lines ~170–1749. Family pig-butchering origin story; Kisújszállás adobe childhood, sawdust stove, outhouse, harvest labor; father’s butcher trade, war service, 1956 aftermath; schooling as “Kish Karikó”; vaccines and public-health campaigns; elementary science clubs; high-school section Three Influences (One/Two/Three subsections ~L1266–1492); path toward University of Szeged.

Part Two — An Extremely Brief Interlude on Science (approx. L1750–1947)

Lines ~1750–1947. Compact genetics primer embedded as the student arrives at university: DNA → mRNA → protein; transcription/translation; why a “cytoplasmic messenger” mattered—apparatus for later mRNA chapters.

Part Three — A Sense of Purpose (approx. L1948–4193)

Lines ~1948–4193. Szeged dorm and exam culture; harvest work alongside study; Béla Francia; Hungarian Academy fellowship; Biological Research Center interferon/2-5A research; illness; Susan’s birth (Zsuzsi); father’s death; 1985 emigration with money sewn into Susan’s teddy bear; Temple University Suhadolnik lab; culture shock; Uniformed Services University postdoc; job search culminating in University of Pennsylvania cardiology/neurosurgery soft-money path.

Part Four — An Outsider Inside the System (approx. L4194–6598)

Lines ~4194–6598. Explicit turn: “the part of my story that journalists later all tend to present”—Penn as cautionary tale across three physician partners. Elliot Barnathan and early therapeutic mRNA ideas; funding hierarchy and Bill Kelley-era medical-school politics; demotions and grant droughts; meeting Drew Weissman at the copier; dendritic-cell vaccine logic; Jesse Gelsinger gene-therapy shadow (1999); immune activation by unmodified RNA; mitochondrial/bacterial/mammalian RNA inflammation screen; pseudouridine / nucleoside modification breakthrough; papers that “the phone didn’t ring” after; RNARX and patent licensing frustrations; eviction from neurosurgery lab space (May 2013).

Part Five — Susan’s Mom (approx. L6599–7199)

Lines ~6599–7199. Parallel identity as Susan Francia’s mother: Penn rowing, Vesper Boat Club, 2008 and 2012 Olympic golds; Karikó’s lab precarity beside her daughter’s ascent; retirement from Penn with adjunct title; looking toward biotech.

Part Six — A Changed World (approx. L7200–8172)

Lines ~7200–8172. BioNTech (Uğur Şahin and Özlem Türeci); industrial mRNA pace vs academia; COVID-19 emergence; fastest-vaccine historical contrast; Pfizer/BioNTech trial success announced weeks after Susan’s wedding; media surge, prizes, Budapest mural, Time 100; letters from people who saw family again after vaccination; Rockefeller honorary degree close.

Epilogue / Photo Insert / Acknowledgments / About the Author

Lines 8173–8329. Epilogue on unfinished discovery and young scientists still starting; photo captions (Hungary through Weissman/BioNTech/awards); Acknowledgments (Ali Benjamin; Madhulika Sikka; named Hungarian and Penn colleagues); About the Author (Hungarian American biochemist; Penn adjunct neurosurgery; foundational work for Pfizer-BioNTech and Moderna COVID mRNA vaccines).
